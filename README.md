### Selected projects

**TIDAS: tsunami detection payload for a 6U CubeSat**  
Winner of the 3rd GISTDA School Satellite Competition (2026). The payload looks for tsunami signals in GNSS ionosphere data and runs a compressed LSTM-CNN model on an STM32H7. Now being developed with GISTDA engineers toward a real flight payload.

**SIRINAPHA: Baan-Pla Link** · [fishsirinapha.com](https://fishsirinapha.com)  
Satellite and AI platform that tells small-scale fishers in the Gulf of Thailand where and when to fish, delivered through LINE. In a Samut Sakhon pilot the fishery suitability index matched real catch locations more than 83% of the time and cut fuel use by 18 to 30%. 1st Runner-Up, Hug Mae Hackathon; 2nd Runner-Up, Thailand ICT Awards 2026.

**SST gap-filling research**  
How cloud-mask shape and persistence affect DINEOF reconstruction of sea surface temperature. Presented at CACS 2026.
